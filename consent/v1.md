# Consent to take part (version 1)

This app is a class project at Boston University. It's used to evaluate a matching
algorithm, and it's open only to invited BU students.

## What we collect
- Your BU email address, used only to sign you in. Other users never see it.
- The profile you write: name, age, height, religion, school, major, job, interests,
  clubs, gender, and what you're looking for.
- Which genders you want to be shown. This is private, and nobody else can see it.
- Photos you upload. We remove location and camera data from them.
- Which profiles you were shown, your ratings of them, and your messages with matches.

## How it's used
Your ratings and photos are used only to evaluate the matching algorithm. They aren't
used to train new models.

## Who can see what
- Other approved users can see your profile and photos only when you're shown to them or
  you've matched. They never see your email, your preferences, or your ratings.
- A match tells both of you only that you both rated each other highly.
- Only the two people in a match can read its messages. If either of you reports the
  other, administrators can read that match's messages to review the report, and every
  such read is logged.
- Administrators see your email and profile when reviewing your account.

## Deleting your data
You can delete your account at any time. That removes your profile, preferences, photos,
ratings, matches, messages, blocks, and the reports you filed. Records of administrator
actions are kept, with your identity removed.
