# Consent to take part (version 2)

This app is a class project at Boston University. It's used to evaluate a matching
algorithm, and it's open only to invited participants.

## What we collect
- Your email address and a password, used only to sign you in. The password is stored
  scrambled, so nobody can read it. Other users never see your email, and we never email you.
- The profile you write: name, age, height, religion (optional), school, major, job,
  interests, clubs, gender, and what you're looking for.
- Which genders you're interested in. This is private, and nobody else can see it. It
  doesn't filter who you see: you'll be shown every other participant, whatever their
  gender, and we record it to test whether our algorithm can learn it from your ratings.
- Which profiles you were shown, how you rated them, how long rating took, your matches,
  and your answers to short "was this worth your time?" questions.

## How it's used
Your ratings are used only to evaluate the matching algorithm. They aren't used to train
new models.

## Who can see what
- Nobody else sees your real name. The study gives you a first name, and other approved
  users see that name and your profile only when you're shown to
  them or you've matched. They never see your email, your preferences, or your ratings.
- A match tells both of you only that you both rated each other highly. There's no
  messaging in the app.
- Administrators see your email and profile when reviewing your account, and the reports
  people make. Every administrator action is logged.
- To study the results, the research team sees every participant's ratings of every other
  participant, with people shown as codes, never names or emails.

## Deleting your data
You can delete your account at any time. That removes your profile, preferences, ratings,
matches, blocks, survey answers, and the reports you filed. Records of administrator
actions are kept, with your identity removed.
