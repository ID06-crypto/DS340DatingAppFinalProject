# Consent to take part (version 3)

This app is a class project at Boston University. It's used to evaluate a matching
algorithm, and it's open only to invited participants.

## What we collect
- Your email address and a password, used only to sign you in. The password is stored
  scrambled, so nobody can read it. Other users never see your email, and we never email you.
- The profile you write: name, age, height, religion (optional), school, major, job,
  interests, clubs, gender, and what you're looking for.
- Which genders you're interested in. This is private, and nobody else can see it. It
  doesn't filter who you see: you'll be shown every other participant, whatever their
  gender, and we record it to test whether our algorithm can learn it from your ratings.
- Which profiles you were shown, how you rated them, how long rating took, your matches,
  and your answers to short "was this worth your time?" questions.
- Whether you asked to see your best match after the study (see below).

## How it's used
Your ratings are used only to evaluate the matching algorithm. They aren't used to train
new models.

## Who can see what
- Nobody else sees your real name, unless you choose the best-match option below. The
  study gives you a first name, and other approved users see that name and your profile
  only when you're shown to them or you've matched. They never see your email, your
  preferences, or your ratings.
- A match tells both of you only that you both rated each other highly. There's no
  messaging in the app.
- Administrators see your email, real name, and profile when reviewing your account, and
  the reports people make. Every administrator action is logged.
- To study the results, the research team sees every participant's ratings of every other
  participant, with people shown as codes, never names or emails.

## Seeing your best match (optional)
After rating closes, we run our algorithm to pair each participant who asked with one
other participant who also asked: their best match. This is entirely optional. Once you're
approved, and before you start rating, we'll ask you to answer yes or no; nothing is
chosen for you. Saying no
doesn't change anything else about taking part.

- If you say yes, and so does the person you're paired with, you'll each see the
  other's real name, study name, and profile. Neither of you sees the other's email or
  ratings, and there's still no messaging in the app.
- It works the same way in both directions. You only see your best match's real name if
  they also agreed to share it, and they only see yours if you agreed. If only one of you
  said yes, neither of you sees the other's name or profile.
- Nobody sees your real name unless they also said yes and were paired with you.
- The algorithm pairs people using everyone's ratings, so being someone's best match
  suggests, but doesn't prove, that you rated each other highly.
- Our group is small, so someone you're shown to might work out who you are from your
  profile, even without your real name.
- You can change your mind on the Me page at any time. If either of you changes to no before
  you've looked, neither of you is shown the other. Once you've seen someone's name, we
  can't make you forget it.

## Deleting your data
You can delete your account at any time. That removes your profile, preferences, ratings,
matches, best match, blocks, survey answers, and the reports you filed. Records of
administrator actions are kept, with your identity removed.
