Definitions

  • A rating is positive at {{ settings.positive_threshold }} or above, on the 1 to 10 scale.
  • A prediction is positive at {{ settings.prediction_threshold }} or above. Matchers predict on a 0 to 1 scale.
  • Only rated impressions count, and opted-out users are left out.
  • Intervals are 95% bootstrap intervals that resample users, not ratings. Each number shows n users and n ratings.
  • Groups with fewer than {{ settings.min_group_size }} users are suppressed. When one group is suppressed, the next smallest is too.
  • Ratings made in under {{ settings.rapid_rating_ms }} ms are flagged, not removed.

Selection bias: an algorithm that chooses who to show is evaluated on the people it chose, so classification metrics for non-random algorithms aren't comparable with the random one. The random algorithm's data is the unbiased sample for offline evaluation.
