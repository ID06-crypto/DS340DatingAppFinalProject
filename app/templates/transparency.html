How this works

What we collect

  • Your email address and a password, only to sign you in. The password is stored scrambled, so nobody can read it. Nobody else using the app sees your email, and we never send you email.
  • The profile you write.
  • Which genders you're interested in. This is private, and it doesn't filter who you see: everyone rates every other participant.
  • Who you were shown, how you rated them, how long rating took, your matches, and your answers to short "was this worth your time?" questions.

Who can see what

  • Other people never see your real name, unless you ask to see your best match (below). The study gives everyone a first name to show instead. They see that name and your profile only when you're shown to them or you've matched. They never see your email, your preferences, your ratings, or whether you rated them, unless you both matched.
  • Your matches see that you matched, until either of you unmatches or blocks. There's no messaging in the app.
  • Administrators see your email and profile when approving accounts, and the reports people make. To study the results, the research team sees a grid of every participant's scores for every other participant, with people shown as codes, never names or emails. Every administrator action is logged.
  • Researchers analyze data with names, emails, religion, and exact ages removed, and with your id replaced by a code.

Your best match (optional)

After rating closes, the algorithm pairs each person who asked with one other person who also asked. If you both ticked the box, you each see the other's real name, study name, and profile on Matches. If either of you unticks it on the Me page, neither of you is shown the other. Leaving it unticked changes nothing else.

Recommendations are an experiment

We're comparing ways of choosing who you see. Each time you're shown someone, one of the algorithms below is picked at random to choose them. Some people deliberately get simple or random recommendations, because that's how we tell whether the others are better. You can opt out on your Me page, and you'll then always get the default algorithm, with your data left out of the analysis.

Deleting your data

You can download your data or delete your account at any time from your Me page. Deleting erases your profile, preferences, ratings, matches, and blocks. Logs of administrator actions are kept, with your identity removed.
