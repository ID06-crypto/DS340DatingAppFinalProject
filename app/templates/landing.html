A study in reciprocal matching

BU matching study

This is a free research project by BU students. It tests whether a matching algorithm can recommend people who like each other, without the tricks many apps use to keep you scrolling.

You'll see one person at a time and rate how interested you are. If two people rate each other highly, they match. There are no streaks, notifications to bring you back, or ads.
